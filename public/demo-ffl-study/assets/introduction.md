# Formula authoring study demo

This study is a runnable reconstruction of the controlled comparison in Section 6 of Wu et al.’s *FFL: A Language and Live Runtime for Styling and Labeling Typeset Math Formulas*.

You will complete four timed formula-augmentation tasks. Some use Formula Formatting Language (FFL), a CSS-like language for styling formulas; others use a LaTeX-style baseline. After every task, you will reflect briefly on the experience. A final open-ended task asks you to use FFL to make a formula easier to understand.

This demo preserves the paper’s task structure and counterbalancing. Its formulas and task targets are representative reconstructions, because the paper’s task screenshots are not included in the publication.
