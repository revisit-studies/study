# Answer Validation and Feedback Demo

Explore correct and incorrect answers, numeric ranges, lists of alternatives, complete selection sets, and custom messages for each attempt.

Each question suggests inputs to try. Start with incorrect answers, click **Check Answer**, then try accepted answers. Counts show how many responses in the current trial are correct and incorrect.

Most pages allow three attempts. After three incorrect checks, the accepted answers appear and you can continue. The final practice page allows unlimited attempts and requires a correct answer before continuing.

The custom feedback page includes a sidebar question. Try one correct and one incorrect response to see counts across both locations.

Most pages show a Check Answer button. The Custom Feedback page demonstrates `hideCheckAnswerButton`: the first Next click checks answers and displays feedback; a later click continues. Most pages explicitly allow continuing after three failed attempts.
