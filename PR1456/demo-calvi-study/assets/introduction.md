# CALVI Mixed Question Bank

This study presents 30 questions from the Critical Thinking Assessment for Literacy in Visualizations (CALVI):

- 15 real visualization-literacy questions
- 15 trick questions

All questions are shown in a single randomized order. Answer each question using the controls in the sidebar.

## Reference

L. W. Ge, Y. Cui, and M. Kay, “CALVI: Critical Thinking Assessment for Literacy in Visualizations,” in *Proceedings of the 2023 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems*, 2023. DOI: [10.1145/3544548.3581406](https://doi.org/10.1145/3544548.3581406)
